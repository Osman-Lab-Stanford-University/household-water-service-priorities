# Measurement and analytical methods

The survey records respondents' reported impact on water-service quality across eight domains using 0–10 integer responses. Each household's mean rating is subtracted to obtain relative-priority ratings. The separate experienced-service deficit measures are oriented so that larger values denote worse conditions.

Exact administered wording, coding decisions and model specifications are described in the Supplementary Information, particularly Tables S3–S5.

## Statistical quantities

- **Reported impact rating:** the administered 0–10 survey response.
- **Relative priority:** the within-household centered impact rating.
- **Differentiation magnitude:** the spread of a household's ratings across domains.
- **Fitted component probability:** the model-based probability used in H3B.
